# DIGITAL MARKETING GROWTH CASE STUDY
## Scaling a Heritage Fine Jewellery Brand into a Modern Omni-Channel Powerhouse

**Client:** ANM JewelleryLine  
**Location:** Shop No. 3, 1st Floor, JMD Kohinoor Mall, Savitri Cinema Road, Greater Kailash Part-II (GK-II), New Delhi, India  
**Industry:** Fine Jewellery, Bridal Trousseau, BIS Hallmarked Gold, Certified Diamonds, Polki & Silver Craftsmanship  
**Target Markets:** Domestic (Delhi NCR, Metros Tier-1) & International NRI Diaspora (USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia)  
**Author / Strategist:** Jiya Batra — Digital Marketer & Growth Strategist  
**Timeframe:** 12-Month Multi-Phase Growth Roadmap  

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

ANM JewelleryLine is an established jewellery house headquartered in the luxury shopping hub of Greater Kailash-II, New Delhi. With a legacy spanning over three decades (established circa late 1980s), the brand specializes in authentic, hallmarked 22k/18k gold jewellery, IGI/GIA certified diamond rings and necklace sets, bridal Kundan/Polki heirlooms, and handcrafted silver articles (payal, kardhani, and contemporary silver jewellery).

Despite superior craftsmanship, verified hallmark credentials, and deep customer trust built through word-of-mouth, ANM JewelleryLine remained virtually invisible across the digital landscape. With 0 direct-to-consumer digital touchpoints, no discoverable e-commerce catalogue, unoptimized search presence, and zero paid acquisition funnels, the brand was leaving immense high-ticket revenue on the table in an era where over 70% of luxury jewellery purchases begin with digital discovery.

This case study details a comprehensive, full-funnel digital marketing transformation designed to scale ANM JewelleryLine from a local South Delhi boutique into an omni-channel, globally recognized jewellery house.

## 04 | PHASE 0 (WEEKS 1–2): DIAGNOSTIC AUDIT & MARKET DISCOVERY

### 1. Brand Discovery & USP Identification
* **Certification Assurance:** 100% BIS Hallmarked gold with laser-inscribed HUID numbers + third-party IGI/GIA certificates for all diamonds.
* **Pricing Advantage:** 15%–25% lower making charges compared to corporate legacy retailers (Hazoorilal, Tanishq) due to direct workshop manufacturing.
* **Bespoke Turnaround:** Rapid 7–14 day custom bridal manufacturing timeline.

### 2. Customer Persona Segmentation
1. **Persona A: The Modern Delhi Bride ("Pooja", 26–32 yrs)**
   * *Profile:* Affluent, educated professional living in South Delhi / Gurugram.
   * *Need:* Contemporary lightweight polki choker sets, pastel enamel jewellery, and custom engagement rings.
   * *Behavior:* Discovers on Instagram Reels & Pinterest, verifies on Google, buys via personal consultation.
2. **Persona B: The Independent Self-Purchasing Woman ("Ritu", 28–45 yrs)**
   * *Profile:* Corporate leader, entrepreneur, high disposable income.
   * *Need:* Everyday diamond pendants, tennis bracelets, minimal stackable rings, office-wear gold earrings.
   * *Behavior:* Seeks transparent pricing, online checkout with insured home delivery.
3. **Persona C: The NRI Diaspora Buyer ("Simran", 27–40 yrs, UK/USA/Canada)**
   * *Profile:* Indian diaspora planning a wedding abroad or returning for wedding shopping.
   * *Need:* Complete bridal trousseau sets, traditional heavy gold jewellery, silver heirloom items.
   * *Behavior:* Demands video-call consultations, door-to-door insured international express shipping.
4. **Persona D: The Festive & Gifting Shopper (Diwali/Akshaya Tritiya/Baby Showers)**
   * *Profile:* Family decision-makers looking for silver *payal*, silver coins, and baby gifts.
