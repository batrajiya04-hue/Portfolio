MEET ANM JEWELLERYLINE

A heritage boutique fine jewellery house from Greater Kailash-II, New Delhi. The mission: Transform an esteemed brick-and-mortar legacy into an omni-channel, globally coveted luxury jewellery label.

✦ 35+ Year Heritage

Established in the late 1980s in South Delhi's luxury shopping corridor (JMD Kohinoor Mall, Savitri Cinema Road, GK-II). Renowned for trusted family generational patronage.

✦ Hallmark & Certified

100% BIS Hallmarked 22K (916) and 18K (750) gold collections, IGI/GIA certified diamond solitaires, bridal Kundan/Polki, and pure silver craftsmanship.

✦ Direct Artisan Edge

In-house master karigar workshops deliver bespoke bridal trousseau in 7-14 days with 15-25% lower making charges than corporate retail chains.
